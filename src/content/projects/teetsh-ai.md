Started as a secret side project prototype that convinced the team to go all-in on AI. Then the real thing, from scratch: the agent (tools, sub-agents, memory, skills, LLM-agnostic core) and the features on top.

- **AI document import**: teachers drop their existing docs and get them rebuilt natively in Teetsh. 10–30% of content creation since day 1.
- **AI onboarding**: an agent that sets up your school year with you. 3x faster activation.
- **Grounded generation**: content generated from the official programmes, with citations.
- **Measurement stack**: tracing, an eval harness with LLM judges, cost and quality dashboards.
