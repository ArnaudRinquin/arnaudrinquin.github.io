It solves two problems: forgetting to play, and scores with nowhere to live. The bot reminds each player privately, collects results by DM or straight from the group chat, and posts a ranked board every night. A Mini App shows today, this week and all time.

LinkedIn games import themselves: the bot reads one player's connections leaderboards, so nobody pastes anything. It all runs on Cloudflare's free tier.
